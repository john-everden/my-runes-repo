# CURRENTS.md
Garden of Freedom – 2026
Dynamic Forces That Shape Garden Movement

------------------------------------------------------------
Overview

Currents describe the invisible forces that move through the Garden.
They are not Modes (states), Strategies (methods), or Connectors (tools).
They are the patterns of motion that influence how threads evolve over time.

Currents operate across all nodes:

Personal ↔ Projects ↔ Community

They interact with the Garden’s core cycle:

qk → evaluation
ᛝ → reframing
TRP‑26 → clarification
k → continuity

Currents are the Garden’s “weather systems.”

------------------------------------------------------------
1. Drift Current (〰️)
Natural Entropy

Purpose:
Acknowledge the natural tendency of meaning, projects, and people to wander unless held with presence.

Effects:
- pulls attention away from anchors
- dissolves clarity
- spreads energy thin
- creates conceptual fog

How to Work With It:
- recognize drift early
- apply Ember Mode (⟡)
- use TRP‑26 to re‑clarify meaning
- reconnect to the Root Node via Spiral Mode (⟲)
- log drift in the ledger

Essence:
〰️ = All things drift. Presence restores direction.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. Convergence Current (⇴)
Alignment of Threads

Purpose:
The force that pulls separate threads toward a shared center.

Effects:
- accelerates progress
- sharpens clarity
- strengthens coherence
- reveals hidden structure

How to Work With It:
- enter Mycelial Mode (⟱)
- map cross‑node connections
- allow threads to merge naturally

Essence:
⇴ = Separate threads find each other.

------------------------------------------------------------

Cycle Initiation

Purpose:
The sudden spark that begins a new cycle.

Effects:

- triggers expansions
- initiates cycles

How to Work With It:
- enter Lantern Mode (✧⃝)
- use Horizon Mode (◌→) to check long‑arc coherence

Essence:
⚡ = A spark becomes a path.

4. Eclipse Current (◐)
Obscured Clarity

Purpose:
The temporary dimming of direction, purpose, or understanding.

Effects:

- slows progress
- invites reflection
- creates uncertainty

How to Work With It:
- enter Stillness Mode (◎)

- wait for the shadow to pass
- hold the smallest truth via Ember Mode (⟡)

Essence:
◐ = When clarity dims, hold the ember.
